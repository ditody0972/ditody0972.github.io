Privacy Policy - prominence-backup

prominence-backup is a personal tool used only by its developer. It accesses only the Google Drive files that it creates itself (drive.file scope), in the developer's own Google account.

It does not collect, sell, share or transfer data of any other person. Backup files are stored only in the developer's own Google Drive. Credentials are stored only on the developer's own servers.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
